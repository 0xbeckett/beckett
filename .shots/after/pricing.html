What we don't charge for.

The costs that are ours to eat, not yours to pay. A coworker who bills you for its own mistakes isn't a coworker.

  • Retries we caused. If Beckett crashed, looped, or re-ran itself because of our bug, that compute is on us.
  • Failed review cycles. When our own reviewer sends work back, the rework isn't a line on your bill.
  • Our overhead. Orchestration, scheduling, the plumbing between agents, that's the cost of running the service, not a task cost.
  • Idle presence. A seat sitting quietly in your Discord doesn't burn credits, the included allowance is for work that ships, not for showing up.
