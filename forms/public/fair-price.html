02 · the rules

What you'd be pricing.

We price pass-through cost plus one stated margin — the platform fee, and only that. Answer for the margin and the seat you'd call fair, not for free.

what's included
  • Model tokens at the provider's published rate, passed straight through.
  • Compute — worker and browser wall-clock at a stated hourly rate.
  • One margin line — the platform fee on top of model + compute.
  • A human seat — $20/mo that includes 200 credits; light use never costs past it.
what isn't
  • Retries we caused. Our bug, our compute — not your line.
  • Failed review cycles. Rework our own reviewer sent back is on us.
  • Our overhead. Orchestration and plumbing aren't a task cost.
  • Idle presence. A quiet seat doesn't meter — you pay when work happens.
